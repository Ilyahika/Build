# V12 species assets

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Not real documentary photographs. Each texture is a 1536 × 1024 image, three columns × two rows, six separate photographic-style species cutouts on a transparent alpha background. Original alpha is retained in the WebP files. Encoded at native resolution using quality 92 and alpha quality 100.

Species-to-cell mapping is explicit in fish_manifest_v12.json. The first four sheets are the pond, the next four the lake, then sea, ocean and stream. The exact generation descriptions (Latin names and anatomy) are in image_prompts_v12.json. Every blank background pixel is transparent; no external images are downloaded at runtime.

Generation constraints: full body, natural colors, accurate real-species anatomy, facing left unless rays/flatfish require dorsal view or seahorse requires upright view, no props, no text, no grid, no landscape. The images are visual game assets and are not a scientific identification reference.
